# Example results

All files in this folder are regenerated by `generate_examples.py`
(fixed seeds — re-run it and the numbers below must reproduce
exactly).  Nothing here is hand-written: the tables are the literal
outputs of `contour-shapes classify` / `batch` on the generated
images.

## Six-shape scene — 6/6 correct

`scene.png` (512×512, seed 99) holds one of each class on a jittered
grid; `output/scene_annotated.png` shows the traced contours (green),
the RDP approximations (yellow) and the `label confidence` tags:

```text
scene.png:0  rectangle conf=0.86 area=7952 circ=0.699 vertices=4 elong=1.56
scene.png:1  circle conf=1.00 area=6364 circ=0.894 vertices=8 elong=1.00
scene.png:2  pentagon conf=0.74 area=5938 circ=0.772 vertices=6 elong=1.01
scene.png:3  ellipse conf=0.74 area=5190 circ=0.725 vertices=8 elong=1.81
scene.png:4  square conf=0.77 area=4800 circ=0.666 vertices=4 elong=1.01
scene.png:5  triangle conf=0.89 area=3628 circ=0.537 vertices=3 elong=1.01
```

Note the pentagon in the scene reports 6 vertices: the scene shapes
are smaller, so the RDP tolerance (2% of a shorter perimeter) is
tighter in absolute pixels and one extra corner survives — the
classifier still labels it correctly (confidence 0.74), because the
vertex term is only one of four.

The same scene with Gaussian noise σ=20 (`scene_noisy.png`) gives
identical labels and confidences to two decimals — Otsu's threshold
on a strongly bimodal histogram is almost unaffected by additive
noise (see `docs/RESEARCH.md` §7).

## Benchmark summary

`contour-shapes evaluate --count 120` (20 images per class, fresh
seeds) — measured 2026-11-25:

| benchmark | accuracy | mean confidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| clean, seed 1000 | 120/120 = 1.0000 | 0.9009 |
| noise σ=10, seed 2000 | 120/120 = 1.0000 | 0.9103 |
| noise σ=20, seed 2000 | 120/120 = 1.0000 | 0.9103 |
| noise σ=30, seed 2000 | 120/120 = 1.0000 | 0.8980 |
| noise σ=50, seed 3000 | 120/120 = 1.0000 | 0.8030 |
| small canvas (128 px) + noise σ=15, 60 images | 60/60 = 1.0000 | — |

Confusion matrices are perfectly diagonal in every run.  Accuracy
holds up to σ=50; what degrades is the mean confidence (0.90 →
0.80), which is the honest signal that the inputs are getting harder.
